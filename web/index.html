Important limitations

  • Calculated concentration is a steady-state average, not a measured trough or post-dose peak.
  • The nonrenal clearance assumption is 40 mL/min; it may vary materially across populations, especially heart failure.
  • Creatinine clearance assumes stable renal function and uses the weight entered; accuracy may be poor at extremes of body composition.
  • Distribution volume is a coarse adult population estimate (age, renal impairment and heart failure modifiers). This is not an individualized PK fit.
  • Do not infer dosing changes or rule out toxicity from these estimates or a single laboratory level.

Model: CrCl = (140 − age) × weight / (72 × serum creatinine), ×0.85 for female coefficient; digoxin CL = 1.303 × CrCl + 40 mL/min. Mean steady-state concentration = F × dose / (clearance in L/h × dosing interval). Parameters are approximations, not personalized predictions.

References: Published Koup–Jusko clearance comparison · Digoxin prescribing information. Measure at least 6 hours after the last dose, ideally immediately before the next dose; interpret in clinical context.
